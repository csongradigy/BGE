Three prices, one contract

S₀: spot today. The price for an immediate transaction at the trade date.

K: delivery price. Agreed now for a compulsory exchange at maturity. It stays fixed for this contract.

Sₜ: spot at maturity. The market price when the contract expires. This is the price you compare with K.

Long: you agree to buy. Payoff per unit = Sₜ − K.

Short: you agree to sell. Payoff per unit = K − Sₜ.

For EUR/HUF, the underlying is EUR and all profits are in HUF. For shares, all profits are in USD. The opening spot price is context and does not enter the terminal forward payoff.

We use a cash-settled illustration. The payment equals the economic payoff of physical delivery. A newly agreed fair forward has zero initial value. This simulator takes its quoted delivery price as given.
